Body fat and diet

Guide: Should I bulk or cut? Calculator for calories and protein

  • Should I bulk or cut at 20 % body fat?

    At 20 % body fat, lose the fat first with a 625 kcal daily deficit. A 180 lb (81.6 kg) man reaches 15 % in about 15 weeks and 10 % in about 33 weeks.

    November 22, 2026Priya Raman
