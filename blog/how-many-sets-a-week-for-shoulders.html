How many sets a week do shoulders need?

Aim for about 10 hard sets a week of lateral raises and upright rows for the side delts. Count bench and overhead pressing for the front delts instead. The 10-set line is the top volume band in the 2017 meta-analysis by Schoenfeld, Ogborn and Krieger. IronQuest's 3-day program gives the side delts 9 sets and a drop set a week. The same program gives the rear delts and the front delts 3 direct sets each.

Aim for about 10 hard sets a week for your side delts. Count lateral raises and upright rows, and leave your pressing out of that count. Bench and overhead press sets go to the front delts. A hard set here means a set that ends 0 to 3 reps short of failure. IronQuest's 3-day program gives the side delts 9 sets and a drop set a week. The same 125 min of training hold 3 direct sets for the rear delts and 3 for the front delts.

How many sets of lateral raises a week?

About 10 hard sets a week of lateral raises and upright rows is the number to start from. It comes from the 2017 meta-analysis by Schoenfeld, Ogborn and Krieger, which pooled 34 training groups from 15 studies. Each extra weekly set went with a gain in muscle size 0.37 % higher. Within the same study, the higher-volume groups had a gain 3.9 % larger than the lower-volume groups. The authors also sorted volume into 3 bands per muscle: fewer than 5, 5 to 9, and 10 or more sets a week. That split only showed a trend, so treat the 10-set line as a sound target and nothing firmer.

The paper counts sets per muscle group and never splits the shoulder into heads. Giving the side delts a count of their own is IronQuest's reading of it. The side delts sit at the outer edge of the shoulder, so they set its width. The shoulder to waist ratio calculator measures that width as shoulder circumference against 1.618 times your waist. For a lifter 1.78 m tall, it puts the golden waist at 79.6 cm and the shoulders at 128.7 cm.

Do pressing sets count toward side-delt volume?

Count pressing for the front delts and leave it out of the side-delt total. IronQuest's exercise library lists the front delts as the main target of the standing dumbbell overhead press, with the side delts as a secondary muscle. For the bench press, the library lists the chest first and the front delts second, with no share of the work for the side delts.

Bench and overhead press sets count toward the front delts here, not toward width. Lateral raises and upright rows are where the side-delt sets come from, and both have the side delts as their main target in the same library. The dumbbell lateral raise and the dumbbell upright row pages list swaps that train the same head, sorted by the equipment you have. If the dumbbell rack is crowded, the one-arm cable lateral raise is a listed alternative in IronQuest's program.

How many weekly sets does each head of the shoulder get?

The side delts get the most, about 10 hard sets a week, and IronQuest gives the rear and front delts 3 direct sets each.

When do more sets stop paying off?

Neither paper reports a weekly set count where growth stopped rising, so 10 sets is a starting point. The 2019 trial by Schoenfeld and colleagues put 34 trained men on 1, 3 or 5 sets per exercise per session. They trained 3 times a week for 8 weeks, so the top group did 15 sets of each exercise a week. Muscle size rose more with higher volume in the elbow flexors and both thigh sites measured.

Strength didn't follow volume. Squat and bench press gains were similar in all 3 groups, and the low-volume sessions took 13 min each. The trial measured the arms and thighs with ultrasound and never measured the shoulders.

That makes extra side-delt sets a size tool. Stay near 10 sets a week while the weight on the raises keeps going up. When you add volume, put it on the side delts first, because width is the goal and the 2019 data tied extra sets to muscle size.

Who shouldn't follow these numbers?

A lifter with a painful shoulder shouldn't use them to decide on more sets. The 2 papers measured muscle growth, and neither tested a sore shoulder, so get a shoulder that hurts during raises or presses checked before you add volume. The 2019 dose was also tested only on trained men, over 8 weeks. A woman, or a lifter in his first weeks of training, sits outside the group that trial measured, and its 15 sets per exercise a week say nothing about either of them.

Questions

How many reps per set for lateral raises?

IronQuest's V-Taper session runs the dumbbell lateral raise at 20, 15 and 10 reps over 3 sets. The load goes up on each set as the reps come down, so set 1 is the lightest of the 3 sets.

Do upright rows count as side-delt sets?

Yes, in IronQuest's count. The exercise library lists the side delts as the main target of the dumbbell upright row. The program's cue stops the pull just under chest height, where the traps start taking over.

Do rows count as rear-delt sets?

IronQuest counts rows as helper work for the rear delts. The exercise library lists the rear delts as a secondary muscle of the seated machine row. The direct rear-delt work is 3 sets of the dumbbell rear-delt raise on Friday.

How long does the Boulder Shoulders session take?

Boulder Shoulders takes 38 min and holds 7 exercises in 6 blocks, from neutral-grip pull-ups to a wrist curl superset. The full IronQuest week of 3 sessions takes 125 min.
