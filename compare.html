The comparisons

IronQuest vs Hevy

Pick Hevy if you already follow a program and want a free log for it on iPhone, Android, Apple Watch or Wear OS, with a social feed and a plate calculator. Pick IronQuest if you want the program, the calorie target and the weekly adjustments handed to you, all aimed at a V-shaped body in about 2 hours of gym a week.

IronQuest vs Strong

Pick Strong if you run your own barbell program and want a plain, fast log on iPhone, Android or Apple Watch, with a plate calculator and a one-time Forever purchase. Pick IronQuest if you want a 3-session program with weight and reps for every set, a calorie target next to it, and a rank that climbs each time you train.

IronQuest vs Fitbod

Pick Fitbod if you want AI-generated workouts built around your own goals, on iPhone, Android or Apple Watch, and you handle your diet elsewhere. Pick IronQuest if you want the same 3 sessions every week, built for a V-shaped body, with a calorie target and a rank system in the same app.

IronQuest vs MacroFactor

Pick MacroFactor if detailed food logging is the job: a large food database, AI photo and barcode logging, micronutrients and calorie targets recalculated every week from your intake and weight, on iPhone or Android. Pick IronQuest if you want the gym program and a simpler calorie and protein target in one app and one subscription, built for a V-shaped body in about 2 hours of gym a week.

IronQuest vs Liftoff

Pick Liftoff if you want your lifts scored and ranked against friends and the world, with free core tracking on iPhone, Android and Apple Watch. Pick IronQuest if you want a rank that rewards showing up, on one program and one diet protocol built for a V-shaped body in about 2 hours of gym a week.

What IronQuest is built for

IronQuest follows one method for one goal: shoulders 1.618 times your waist. The program is 3 sessions a week, V-Taper (44 min), Iron Legs (43 min) and Boulder Shoulders (38 min), with weight and reps on every set. The diet runs on a protocol set by your body type (build, recomp or cut), and a weekly check-in adjusts your daily calorie and protein target. Sessions, steps and quests pay XP that moves your rank from E to S.

IronQuest runs on iPhone only, has no free plan and does not generate programs with AI. If you train with an Android phone, want a free log or want a new workout every day, one of the apps above fits you better.
