How the load goes up

Every exercise has 3 sets with a fixed rep count that goes down, for example 10, 8 and 6 reps. Set 1 is the lightest. Set 2 aims at set 1 plus 10 %, set 3 at set 2 plus 10 %, each rounded to the nearest step: 2.5 kg on a barbell, 2 kg on dumbbells, 2.5 kg on a cable and 5 kg on a machine stack (5 lb, 5 lb, 5 lb and 10 lb in pounds). When 10 % is smaller than one step, the set still goes up by one step, so the ladder always climbs. On the big lifts, set 3 stops 1 rep before your form breaks.

Progress is counted per set. When a set reaches its reps with clean form, that set goes up one step at the next session, usually 1.25 kg per side on a barbell. Set 3 can move while set 1 stays where it is. Holding the same numbers for 2 or 3 weeks is normal. If all 3 sets of a big lift stay frozen for about 2 weeks, take its swap for a few weeks, then come back to it.

One week, worked example

Monday, V-Taper: incline dumbbell press with 20 kg dumbbells on set 1. The tool fills 10 reps at 20 kg, 8 reps at 22 kg and 6 reps at 24 kg. All 3 sets are clean, so next Monday reads 22, 24 and 26 kg. The lateral raises at 6 kg show the step rule: 6, 8 and 10 kg, because 10 % of 6 kg is less than a 2 kg step.

Wednesday, Iron Legs: high-bar squat at 60 kg gives 60, 65 and 72.5 kg for 10, 8 and 6 reps. Set 3 stops at 5 reps, so next week sets 1 and 2 move to 62.5 and 67.5 kg and set 3 stays at 72.5 kg until it gets its 6 reps.

Friday, Boulder Shoulders: standing dumbbell overhead press at 16 kg gives 16, 18 and 20 kg. In pounds, a 135 lb squat reads 135, 150 and 165 lb.

Who it is for

People who can train 3 days a week with a rest day between sessions, for example Monday, Wednesday and Friday, and who want a V shape: wide shoulders and back over a narrow waist. The upper body gets 2 sessions and the legs get 1. You need a barbell, dumbbells, a cable station, a pull-up bar and the usual leg machines, and every exercise with a busy or missing machine has a swap. The target is shoulders 1.618 times the waist: the golden ratio calculator gives your numbers, and the bulk or cut calculator sets the calories that go with the program.

Limits of the tool

The weights it fills come from the rule above. They do not test your strength, and dumbbell racks and machine stacks differ from gym to gym, so round to what yours has. The IronQuest app keeps every set in a log, with the weight and reps of each one.

Questions

Is 3 days a week enough to build muscle?

Yes. A 2016 meta-analysis by Schoenfeld, Ogborn and Krieger found more muscle growth when a muscle group is trained twice a week than once. This program trains the chest, back, shoulders and arms twice, in V-Taper and Boulder Shoulders, and the legs once, hard, in Iron Legs.

What weight should I start with?

For set 1, take a weight you could lift 2 or 3 more times than the program asks: for 10 reps, a weight you could move 12 times with clean form. Your first 2 sessions are for finding it. Start light and add load rather than failing in week 1.

What if I cannot do a pull-up?

Tap Swap and take the cable lat pulldown, with a bar on Monday and a V-bar on Friday. When set 1 of the pulldown reaches your bodyweight on the stack, open the next session with a pull-up test: 10 clean reps on Monday or 12 on Friday and the slot becomes pull-ups.

Do I need cardio on top of the 3 sessions?

No cardio sessions. The program counts on daily steps, with 10,000 steps a day as the mark, on training days and rest days.

Sources
