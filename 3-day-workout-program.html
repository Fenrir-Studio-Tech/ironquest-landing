The 3-day gym program for a V-shaped body: 2 hours a week

The program is 3 gym sessions a week: V-Taper (44 min), Iron Legs (43 min) and Boulder Shoulders (38 min), 125 minutes in total. Each session has 5 blocks of 3 sets, and inside a block the reps go down while the load goes up.

Updated November 16, 2026

Sessions
3 a week, with a rest day between them
Time
44 + 43 + 38 min = 125 min a week
Volume
5 blocks per session, 3 sets per exercise
Reps
Fixed and descending, for example 10 / 8 / 6
Progression
Set by set, by the smallest plate available
Goal
Shoulders 1.618 times the waist (golden ratio calculator)

How the sets work

Every exercise is 3 sets with a fixed rep count per set. Set 1 is the lightest load at the highest rep count. Each following set adds about 10 % of load for fewer reps, so the heaviest set comes last. On the big lifts, the last set stops 1 rep short of the point where your form breaks.

A line such as 10 / 8 / 6 reads as 10 reps on set 1, 8 reps on set 2 and 6 reps on set 3. Rest times are in minutes and seconds. A drop set means that right after set 3 you lower the load and do as many reps as you can.

The progression rule

Each set has its own load and moves on its own. When a set reaches its rep count with clean form, the load of that set goes up by the smallest step available at the next session, usually 1.25 kg per side on a barbell. Set 3 can go up while set 1 stays where it is.

Holding the same numbers for 2 or 3 weeks is normal. If all 3 sets of a big lift stay frozen for about 2 weeks, switch to the alternative in the table for a few weeks, then come back to the original lift. If an exercise hurts a joint or a tendon, stop it and take the alternative.

The pull-up variants

Both upper-body sessions open at the pull-up bar. If you cannot do the first set at bodyweight with a full range of motion, 10 reps in V-Taper or 12 reps in Boulder Shoulders, take the lat pulldown for that slot. A pulldown starts at 5 kg, so the load goes up in small steps from the first session.

  • Back to pull-ups. When set 1 of the pulldown reaches your bodyweight on the stack, start the next session with a bodyweight pull-up test. Hit the rep count of set 1 clean and the slot becomes pull-ups.
  • Adding weight. In Boulder Shoulders, sets 2 and 3 stay at bodyweight until you get 12 / 10 / 8 clean. The belt comes after that.

Squat, leg press or trap-bar deadlift

The first lift of Iron Legs is your choice between the high-bar squat, the 45° leg press and the trap-bar deadlift. The second lift follows from it. After a squat or a leg press comes the dumbbell Romanian deadlift. After a trap-bar deadlift come stationary dumbbell lunges, feet planted between reps, with the reps counted per leg: a set of 15 means 15 on each side.

Warm-up

Before the first big lift of every session, ramp up: start with an empty bar or a light pair of dumbbells, add load in steps and cut the reps as you get close to the weight of set 1. Before Iron Legs, add hip and ankle mobility work on the floor. Before the two upper-body sessions, light shoulder rotations with a band or a cable and a light set of lateral raises are enough.

What the program leaves out

There is no triceps isolation and no ab exercise. The triceps work in the incline press, the overhead press and the bench press, and the abs brace every standing lift. Visible abs come from a low body fat, which is the job of the diet: see build, recomp or cut.

Questions

Is 3 days a week enough to build muscle?

The program is built on 3 sessions on purpose. Each one is anchored on 2 heavy compound lifts where you add load set by set, and the rest day between sessions leaves your nervous system time to recover. The key lifts across the week are the weighted pull-up, the incline press, the squat, the overhead press and the bench press.

Which days should I train?

Any 3 days with a rest day between them, for example Monday, Wednesday and Friday. Pick the slots that survive your real schedule, because showing up every week counts for more than the hour of the day.

What if I cannot do pull-ups?

Take the lat pulldown in the same slot: with a bar in V-Taper, with a V-bar in Boulder Shoulders. When your first set reaches your bodyweight on the stack, open the next session with a pull-up test. If you get 10 clean reps in V-Taper or 12 in Boulder Shoulders, you switch to pull-ups.

Why is there only one leg day?

The target is a V shape, with shoulders 1.618 times the waist. Legs are trained hard once a week, with a squat or a deadlift, a hamstring lift, calves and a curl and extension superset, which keeps them in proportion with the upper body.

Do I need cardio on top of the 3 sessions?

No cardio sessions. The program counts on daily steps, with 10,000 steps a day as the mark to aim for. In IronQuest every milestone from 2,500 to 20,000 steps pays XP.

When do the results show?

The loads go up from the first weeks. The change in the mirror takes about 3 months, and by about 6 months it is unmistakable. Sleep, a diet matched to your goal and a training log explain most stalls.
