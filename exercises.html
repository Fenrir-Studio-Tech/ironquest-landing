What the library holds

The library is the exercise list of the IronQuest app: 4,510 exercises, each with a demonstration image, the body part it trains, the equipment it needs and its movement type, such as compound, isolation, calisthenics, stretch or cardio.

The 786 most popular have their own page with the target and secondary muscles, the muscle split and up to 8 alternatives, and 667 of those pages carry a demonstration video. The other 3,724 open a short card right here.

The muscle split is the share of the work each muscle does in an exercise. On the barbell bench press, the chest does 60 % of the work, the front delts 20 % and the triceps 20 %.

Questions

How do I find exercises for one muscle?

Tap the muscle group in the row under the search box. The library holds 424 chest exercises and 658 back exercises.

What does an exercise page show?

The 786 most popular exercises have a full page with the demo, the muscles and the alternatives. The other 3,724 open a short card here with the image, the body part, the equipment and the movement type.

Can I train at home with only dumbbells or bodyweight?

Yes. Type dumbbell or bodyweight in the search box. The library holds 591 dumbbell exercises and 2,051 bodyweight ones.
