Questions

What muscles does the flutter kicks (version 3) work?

The flutter kicks (version 3) targets the quads, and it also works the adductors and hip abductors. The split is 60 % quads, 22 % adductors and 18 % hip abductors.

What equipment do you need for the flutter kicks (version 3)?

You do the flutter kicks (version 3) with your body weight, so it needs no equipment. Alternatives on this page use dumbbells instead.

What can I do instead of the flutter kicks (version 3)?

The closest swaps for the flutter kicks (version 3) are the lying simultaneous alternating straight leg raise, the seated flutter kick and the lying alternate leg extension. The alternatives section lists all 8, closest first.
