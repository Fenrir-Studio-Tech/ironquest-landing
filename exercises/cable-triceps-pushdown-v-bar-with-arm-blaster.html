Questions

What muscles does the cable triceps pushdown (v bar) (with arm blaster) work?

The cable triceps pushdown (v bar) (with arm blaster) targets the triceps. All the work goes to the triceps.

What equipment do you need for the cable triceps pushdown (v bar) (with arm blaster)?

The cable triceps pushdown (v bar) (with arm blaster) needs a cable machine. Alternatives on this page use a resistance band, a lever machine and your body weight instead.

What can I do instead of the cable triceps pushdown (v bar) (with arm blaster)?

The closest swaps for the cable triceps pushdown (v bar) (with arm blaster) are the cable triceps pushdown (v bar attachment), the resistance band triceps pushdown and the cable reverse grip triceps pushdown (with arm blaster). The alternatives section lists all 8, closest first.
