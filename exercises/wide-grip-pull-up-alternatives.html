Wide grip pull-up alternatives

The closest alternatives to the wide grip pull-up are the pull-up (neutral grip), the weighted pull-up and the assisted pull-up. Each one is a vertical pull for the lats, and the list covers 4 kinds of equipment.

Updated November 16, 2026

The wide grip pull-up is a vertical pull done with bodyweight. Most of the work goes to the lats, with help from the biceps, forearms and traps. A vertical pull brings a handle down from overhead, or the body up to a bar.

IronQuest lists 8 equivalents for the wide grip pull-up: 4 with bodyweight, 2 with added weight, 1 on a machine and 1 with a resistance band. All 8 stay in the pull-up variation.

When to swap the wide grip pull-up

The wide grip pull-up needs no station, so there is rarely a queue for it. In a gym, the weighted pull-up trains the lats with added weight, with a load you can raise from one session to the next.

If the wide grip pull-up is too hard for now, the assisted pull-up is the easier vertical pull to build up with.
