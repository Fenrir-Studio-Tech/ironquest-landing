Questions

What muscles does the neck extension stretch work?

The neck extension stretch targets the rotator cuff, and it also works the traps. The split is 70 % rotator cuff and 30 % traps.

What equipment do you need for the neck extension stretch?

You do the neck extension stretch with your body weight, so it needs no equipment.

What can I do instead of the neck extension stretch?

The closest swaps for the neck extension stretch are the lying neck extension stretch, the forward flexion neck stretch and the extension and inclination neck stretch. The alternatives section lists all 8, closest first.
