Questions

What muscles does the lever seated hip adduction work?

The lever seated hip adduction targets the adductors. All the work goes to the adductors.

What equipment do you need for the lever seated hip adduction?

The lever seated hip adduction needs a lever machine. Alternatives on this page use your body weight, a resistance band and a cable machine instead.

What can I do instead of the lever seated hip adduction?

The closest swaps for the lever seated hip adduction are the lever side hip adduction, the bodyweight kneeling butterfly with towels and the band seated hip internal rotation. The alternatives section lists all 8, closest first.
