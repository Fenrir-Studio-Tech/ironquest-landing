Cable Kneeling Rear Delt Row

The cable kneeling rear delt row is a pull exercise for the rear delts, done on a cable machine. It also works the traps, biceps, side delts, rotator cuff and forearm flexors.

Questions

What muscles does the cable kneeling rear delt row work?

The cable kneeling rear delt row targets the rear delts, and it also works the traps, biceps, side delts, rotator cuff and forearm flexors. The split is 46 % rear delts, 16 % traps, 12 % biceps and 26 % across 3 more muscles.

What equipment do you need for the cable kneeling rear delt row?

The cable kneeling rear delt row needs a cable machine. Alternatives on this page use dumbbells, a barbell, a resistance band and a Smith machine instead.

What can I do instead of the cable kneeling rear delt row?

The closest swaps for the cable kneeling rear delt row are the cable standing rear delt row, the dumbbell rear delt row_shoulder and the barbell rear delt row. The alternatives section lists all 8, closest first.
