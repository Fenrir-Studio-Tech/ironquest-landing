Flutter kick alternatives

The closest alternatives to the flutter kick are the lying simultaneous alternating straight leg raise, the dumbbell seated military hold alternate leg raise on floor and the seated flutter kick. All 8 alternatives on this page are leg raises that work the quads.

Updated November 16, 2026

The flutter kick is a leg raise done with bodyweight. Most of the work goes to the quads, with help from the adductors and hip abductors. A leg raise brings the legs toward the torso while the upper body stays in place. The two sides take turns inside the set.

IronQuest lists 8 equivalents for the flutter kick: 7 with bodyweight and 1 with dumbbells. All 8 stay in the kicks variation.

When to swap the flutter kick

The flutter kick needs no station, so there is rarely a queue for it. In a gym, the dumbbell seated military hold alternate leg raise on floor trains the quads with dumbbells, with a load you can raise from one session to the next.
