Smith machine bench press alternatives

The closest alternatives to the Smith machine bench press are the bench press, the dumbbell bench press and the cable bench press. Each one is a chest press for the chest, and the list covers 7 kinds of equipment.

Updated November 16, 2026

The Smith machine bench press is a chest press done on a Smith machine. Most of the work goes to the chest, with help from the front delts and triceps. A chest press pushes the load away from the chest, lying on a bench, seated or in a push-up.

IronQuest lists 8 equivalents for the Smith machine bench press: 1 with a barbell, 2 with dumbbells, 1 on a cable, 1 with a resistance band, 1 on a machine, 1 with a kettlebell and 1 on a Smith machine. All 8 stay in the flat variation.

When to swap the Smith machine bench press

If the Smith machine is taken at peak hours, move to the bench press. It is done with a barbell and works the chest through the same movement.

At home, the band bench press needs only a resistance band.

If the fixed path of the machine irritates a wrist, an elbow or a shoulder, the dumbbell bench press lets each arm move on its own line.
