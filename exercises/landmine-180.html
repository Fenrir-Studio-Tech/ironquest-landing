Questions

What muscles does the landmine 180 work?

The landmine 180 targets the obliques, and it also works the abs. The split is 70 % obliques and 30 % abs.

What equipment do you need for the landmine 180?

The landmine 180 needs a barbell. Alternatives on this page use a medicine ball, added weight, dumbbells, a kettlebell and a resistance band instead.

What can I do instead of the landmine 180?

The closest swaps for the landmine 180 are the Russian twist (with medicine ball), the weighted Russian twist (legs up) and the dumbbell Russian twist. The alternatives section lists all 8, closest first.
