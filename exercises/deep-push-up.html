The deep push up is a calisthenics push exercise for the chest, done with dumbbells. It also works the triceps, front delts and biceps.

Questions

What muscles does the deep push up work?

The deep push up targets the chest, and it also works the triceps, front delts and biceps. The split is 55 % chest, 22 % triceps, 18 % front delts and 5 % biceps.

What equipment do you need for the deep push up?

The deep push up needs dumbbells. Alternatives on this page use a kettlebell, added weight, a resistance band, your body weight, a medicine ball, a barbell and a lever machine instead.

What can I do instead of the deep push up?

The closest swaps for the deep push up are the kettlebell deep push-up, the weighted push-up with vest and the resistance band push-up. The alternatives section lists all 8, closest first.
