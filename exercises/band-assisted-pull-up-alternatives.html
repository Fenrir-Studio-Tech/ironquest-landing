Band assisted pull-up alternatives

The closest alternatives to the band assisted pull-up are the assisted pull-up, the weighted pull-up and the pull-up. The 8 alternatives on this page work the lats on a machine, with added weight or with bodyweight.

Updated November 16, 2026

The band assisted pull-up is a vertical pull done with a resistance band. Most of the work goes to the lats, with help from the biceps, forearms, rear delts and rotator cuff. A vertical pull brings a handle down from overhead, or the body up to a bar.

IronQuest lists 8 equivalents for the band assisted pull-up: 3 on a machine, 2 with added weight and 3 with bodyweight. All 8 stay in the pull-up variation. The pull-up is the one that needs no equipment.

When to swap the band assisted pull-up

The band assisted pull-up needs no station, so there is rarely a queue for it. In a gym, the weighted pull-up trains the lats with added weight, with a load you can raise from one session to the next.

At home with no equipment, the pull-up is the bodyweight version of the movement.
