Who writes the program

Hevy is a log first. You build routines yourself or copy one from Hevy's Routine Library, and a program tailored to you, Hevy Trainer, is listed under Hevy Pro on the App Store.

IronQuest starts from one goal, shoulders 1.618 times your waist, and gives every player the same week: V-Taper (44 min), Iron Legs (43 min) and Boulder Shoulders (38 min). The exercises follow your answers (pull-ups or pulldowns, squat or leg press, a sensitive back or knees), and each set arrives with its weight and reps. If you prefer your own split, IronQuest also has a program builder with 10 splits, supersets, circuits and 12 set types.

Diet

Hevy's site and App Store page list no calorie or macro feature, so a Hevy user who wants a diet plan runs a second app next to it.

IronQuest sets a protocol from your body type (build, recomp or cut), then a daily calorie and protein target and an eating window. Once a week you weigh in and take 4 tape measurements, and IronQuest moves the target when your body drifts from the projection. A recipe video shared from TikTok comes back as a recipe with its kcal and protein per portion.

Devices, price and extras

Hevy runs on Android, Wear OS, Apple Watch and the web. IronQuest is built for iPhone, with the rest timer in the Dynamic Island and on the lock screen and sessions that keep working offline.

Hevy is free with no ads, and its paid tier has a lifetime option. IronQuest is free for 14 days on every plan, then one monthly or yearly subscription for the program, the diet and the game.

Hevy has a plate calculator, a warm-up set calculator and a social feed. IronQuest hands you the weight and reps of every set and swaps a taken machine for an equivalent exercise among 4,510.

What keeps you coming back

Hevy's motivation comes from other lifters: you follow athletes, like and comment on their workouts and save their routines.

In IronQuest the reward is your own rank. Sessions, daily steps and quests pay XP that moves you from rank E up to rank S. A weekly streak survives a missed week with a freeze, 59 achievement cards are graded E to S, and your 3D character earns its outfits through training.

What Hevy leaves to you

  • Hevy logs what you lift and leaves the program to you unless you pay for Hevy Pro. IronQuest gives every player a 3-session program with the weight and reps of every set.
  • Hevy lists no calorie, macro or recipe feature. IronQuest sets a daily calorie and protein target from your body type and adjusts it every week from your weigh-in and tape measurements.
  • Hevy lists no XP or ranks, and its motivation is other lifters' workouts in a feed. IronQuest pays XP for sessions, steps and quests and moves your own rank from E to S.

Pick IronQuest if

  • You want to be told what to lift: 3 sessions of 38 to 44 minutes, with weight and reps for every set.
  • Your goal is how you look, broad shoulders over a narrow waist, more than a bigger total on the bar.
  • You want the calorie and protein target in the same app as the training, adjusted every week from your weigh-in and tape measurements.
  • A rank, quests and a character you dress by training would get you to the gym more often than a feed of other lifters.

Questions about IronQuest and Hevy

Is Hevy free?

Yes. Hevy's App Store page describes the app as free with no ads. Hevy Pro adds unlimited routines and custom exercises, and the US App Store lists it at $2.99 and $3.99 monthly, $23.99 yearly and $74.99 lifetime (checked 16 Nov 2026).

Does Hevy track calories or macros?

Hevy's site and App Store page list no nutrition feature (checked 16 Nov 2026). IronQuest sets a daily calorie and protein target from your body type and adjusts it at a weekly check-in.

Is IronQuest available on Android or Apple Watch?

IronQuest runs on iPhone, with the rest timer in the Dynamic Island and on the lock screen and sessions that keep working offline. Hevy runs on iPhone, iPad, Android, Apple Watch, Wear OS and the web.

Is IronQuest a good Hevy alternative if I only want to log my own program?

Yes. IronQuest has a program builder with 10 splits, supersets, circuits and 12 set types, and one tap logs a set and starts the rest timer. On top of the log you get the 3-session program, the diet protocol and the rank system, free for 14 days on every plan.

Can I try IronQuest for free?

Yes. Every plan starts with 14 days free. Then one monthly or yearly subscription covers the program, the diet and the game.
