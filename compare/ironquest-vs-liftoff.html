What the rank measures

Liftoff's rank is a strength score. Each set you log gets a rating from 1 to 1,000 that accounts for your bodyweight and sex, and the rating places the lift on one of nine ranks, from Wood up to Olympian. Leaderboards compare you with friends, your country and the world in weekly cohorts. A stronger lifter ranks higher.

An IronQuest rank, from E to S, is built from XP. Sessions, daily step milestones from 2,500 to 20,000 steps and quests all pay XP, so a beginner who trains 3 times a week and walks every day climbs the ranks without a heavy bench press. Your physique is measured separately, with a tape, against the 1.618 shoulder-to-waist ratio.

The program

Liftoff's generator builds sessions and multi-week programs from your goal, schedule, equipment and experience, so two Liftoff users can train in very different ways.

IronQuest has one program for one goal. The week is V-Taper (44 min), Iron Legs (43 min) and Boulder Shoulders (38 min). Your answers pick the exercises, every set shows its weight and reps, and you can swap to an equivalent exercise among 4,510 when a machine is taken.

Nutrition

Liftoff logs food: its site lists an AI photo scanner, barcode scanning, food search, custom meals and macro goals, with AI food scanning among the paid Liftoff Pro extras. Recipes are not mentioned on Liftoff's site.

IronQuest gives you the target as well as the log. Your body type sets a protocol (build, recomp or cut) with a daily calorie and protein target, a weekly check-in with a weigh-in and 4 tape measurements adjusts it, and recipes shared from TikTok arrive with kcal and protein per portion.

Where Liftoff is ahead

Liftoff runs on Android and Apple Watch. IronQuest runs on iPhone only and has no watch app.

Core workout tracking in Liftoff is free. IronQuest has no free plan: it is a subscription with a 14-day trial.

Liftoff has leaderboards and friends, Strava sharing, and CSV import of your history from Strong or Hevy. IronQuest has no leaderboard and no import.

Mascot and character

Liftoff's App Store notes name an elephant coach, Jymbo, and quests pay eggs that buy cosmetics, gear and workout plans. In IronQuest you have a 3D character of your own, its outfits are earned by training, and 59 achievement cards graded E to S record what you have done.

Pick Liftoff if

  • You want to know how strong your bench or squat is next to other lifters of your bodyweight.
  • You like competing with friends on a leaderboard.
  • You train with an Android phone or an Apple Watch.
  • You want free workout tracking.
  • You are moving from Strong or Hevy and want to import your history.

Pick IronQuest if

  • You care more about your shoulder-to-waist ratio than about your strength ranking.
  • You want a rank you can climb as a beginner, by training 3 times a week and walking.
  • You want one program, about 2 hours of gym a week, with weight and reps on every set.
  • You want a calorie and protein target that moves with your weekly weigh-in and tape measurements.

Questions about IronQuest and Liftoff

What is the difference between Liftoff ranks and IronQuest ranks?

Liftoff ranks strength: each set gets a rating from 1 to 1,000 adjusted for bodyweight and sex, on nine ranks from Wood to Olympian. IronQuest ranks run from E to S and climb with XP earned from sessions, steps and quests.

Is Liftoff free?

Liftoff's site says the app is free to download and core workout tracking is free. Liftoff Pro adds extras such as deeper rank analytics, long-timeframe charts and AI food scanning, and the US App Store lists it at prices from $3.99 to $79.99 without naming each billing period (checked 16 Nov 2026).

Does IronQuest have leaderboards like Liftoff?

No. IronQuest has no leaderboard. Your rank, streak and achievement cards track your own progress.

Which app is better for building a V-taper?

IronQuest is built for that goal: its program, its diet protocol and its weekly tape measurements all target shoulders 1.618 times the waist. Liftoff builds programs for the goal you choose and scores how strong you are.

Is IronQuest on Android?

No. IronQuest runs on iPhone only. Liftoff is on the App Store and Google Play.
