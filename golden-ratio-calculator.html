How it works

The calculator multiplies your waist by 1.618 to get the shoulder circumference to aim for. Your current ratio is your shoulders divided by your waist. Both are circumferences: the waist at its narrowest point, the shoulders around the deltoids where they are widest.

A ratio alone says nothing about size, since a 40 in waist with 64.7 in shoulders also scores 1.618. So the calculator anchors the waist to your height too. IronQuest uses the same 1.618 target and the same height rule: you get the pair for your height during setup, and the app puts your tape numbers next to it at every weekly check-in.

Worked example

A man 5 ft 10 in (178 cm) tall, with a 33 in waist and 47 in shoulders. His target is 33 × 1.618 = 53.4 in, so he has 6.4 in to add around the shoulders. His ratio is 47 ÷ 33 = 1.424. For his height the golden waist is 31.3 in, 1.7 in under his waist, and the golden shoulders are 50.6 in. The tool opens with these numbers.

How to measure your waist and shoulders

Use a soft tape and stand relaxed with your arms at your sides. Keep the tape flat on the skin and level with the floor, and read it after a normal breath out without pulling it tight.

  • Waist: at the narrowest point, usually a little above the navel.
  • Shoulders: around the deltoids at their widest, over the top of the chest and the upper back. A mirror or a second person helps keep the tape level.

Measure once a week, on the same day, in the morning before you eat. Food and water move the waist reading through the day.

How to close the gap

The shoulder tape grows with the deltoids, the upper chest and the upper back. Limits

1.618 comes from the golden ratio in mathematics, and no study has tested it as the best shoulder to waist ratio. The research on how men’s bodies are rated points the same way: in Maisey and colleagues (1999), the waist to chest ratio predicted women’s ratings of men better than BMI did, and Sell and colleagues (2017) found that cues of upper body strength explained most of the variance in ratings. IronQuest sets the golden waist at 0.447 times your height. For a health screen, use the waist-to-height ratio in the BMI calculator.

Questions

What is the golden ratio for shoulders to waist?

It is 1.618: your shoulder circumference divided by your waist circumference, both taken with a tape. A 32 in waist calls for 51.8 in around the shoulders, and a 34 in waist for 55.0 in.

Is the ratio measured with shoulder width or circumference?

With circumference. The tape goes around the deltoids where the shoulders are widest. A shoulder width taken straight across from bone to bone gives a much smaller number and does not work with 1.618.

What is the ideal waist for my height?

IronQuest sets the golden waist at 0.447 times your height: 80.5 cm for a height of 180 cm, and 31.3 in for a height of 5 ft 10 in (70 in).

Can I reach the golden ratio by losing fat alone?

Yes, if your shoulders already match the golden shoulders for your height. Enter your height and compare the two. If they match, only the waist has to come down, and the waist follows your body fat.
