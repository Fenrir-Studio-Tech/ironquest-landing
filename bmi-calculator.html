How it works

BMI divides your weight in kilograms by your height in metres squared. The WHO reads the result on 6 adult bands: under 18.5 kg/m² is underweight, 18.5 to 24.9 kg/m² is normal weight, 25 to 29.9 kg/m² is overweight, and obesity starts at 30 kg/m², with class II from 35 kg/m² and class III from 40 kg/m². In pounds and inches, multiply by 703: 703 × 176 lb ÷ (70 in × 70 in) gives 25.3 kg/m².

The waist-to-height ratio divides your waist by your height, both in cm or both in inches. A 2010 review by Browning, Hsieh and Ashwell settled on 0.5 as the boundary for men and women alike, which means keeping your waist under half your height.

The body-fat estimate is relative fat mass (RFM), a formula Woolcott and Bergman fitted in 2018 on DXA scans of American adults: 64 − 20 × height ÷ waist for men, 76 − 20 × height ÷ waist for women, in percent. IronQuest uses the same formula when you log your waist at the weekly check-in.

Worked example

A man of 178 cm and 80 kg with an 86 cm waist:

  • BMI: 80 kg ÷ (1.78 m × 1.78 m) = 25.2 kg/m², overweight by 0.2 kg/m².
  • Waist-to-height ratio: 86 cm ÷ 178 cm = 0.48, under 0.5.
  • Body fat: 64 − 20 × 178 cm ÷ 86 cm = 64 − 41.4 = 22.6 %. A woman with the same numbers reads 34.6 %.

His BMI sits 0.2 kg/m² over the overweight line while his waist stays 3 cm under half his height, so his extra weight is not mainly around his waist.

Limits

BMI counts muscle as weight like any other. A lifter at 178 cm and 88 kg reads 27.8 kg/m², overweight, even with an 82 cm waist, a waist-to-height ratio of 0.46 and an estimated 20.6 % body fat.

The WHO bands are for adults. From 5 to 19 years, the WHO reads BMI for age against its growth reference instead. RFM was fitted on Mexican-American, European-American and African-American adults, and its paper says nothing about other groups.

The tape moves the estimate: at 178 cm, a waist 2 cm bigger adds 0.9 points of body fat. Measure at the same spot each time.

Questions

What is a healthy BMI for my height?

The WHO normal-weight band runs from 18.5 to 24.9 kg/m². At 178 cm that is 58.6 to 78.9 kg. At 5 ft 10 in it is 129 to 174 lb.

Is BMI accurate if I lift weights?

It reads all weight the same, so added muscle pushes it up. Check your waist-to-height ratio next to it: under 0.5 with a BMI over 25 usually means the extra weight is not around your waist.

What waist-to-height ratio should I aim for?

Under 0.5, so a waist under half your height. That is under 89 cm at 178 cm, or under 35 in at 5 ft 10 in.

How accurate is the body-fat estimate?

It comes from 2 numbers, your height and your waist, fitted on DXA scans of more than 16,000 American adults. A DXA scan stays more precise. The estimate works best for following your own trend with the tape at the same spot each week.
